Institutional Security & HIPAA Compliance Norms

1. Protected Health Information (PHI) Handling

All clinical charts, lab results, telemetry waveforms, and patient records are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3 with forward secrecy.

2. Audit Trails & Session Monitoring

In accordance with 45 CFR § 164.312(b), each transaction, login attempt, medical chart view, and prescription change is immutably logged with timestamp, workstation IP, and digital signature.

3. Automatic Inactivity Logout

Workstation sessions automatically terminate after 15 minutes of inactivity on non-dedicated clinical terminals.

4. Emergency Access (Break-Glass Protocol)

Emergency medical overrides trigger immediate notification to the Hospital Information Security Officer (CISO) and require clinical justification within 2 hours of incident.
